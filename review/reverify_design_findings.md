## Main assessment

The shortage is meaningful successive demands rather than a fast simulation clock. Instant deployment and quick packet feedback can remain. First stabilize correctness and rescue an affordable opening; then make contracts pay for fresh work, distinguish research investments, and test a supplied final operation with alternative strategies. A longer Cash counter or slower animation will add waiting without ensuring better play.

The strongest new source findings are shared Intel/Crypto saturation, a stale six-tile Intel sweep claim, hot Trace compounding per hop, and Notoriety's stale strength claim. Some are rule mismatches with legitimate alternative resolutions; choose a rule and verify both the actual mechanic and its explanation. Do not classify every optional branch or fixed numeric balance as a bug.
