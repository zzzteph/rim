# Simulation re-verification, RIM v0.5.8

Reviewed the current `scripts/main.gd` on 2026-10-02. The comparison with `review/snapshot/scripts/main.gd` shows drawing, redraw and visibility-cache changes, but no change to the simulation functions responsible for the findings below. Old probe output was not used as current evidence.

The private current copy is `review/reverify_sim/`. Its `Session.SAVE_PATH` and `Settings.PATH` point to `res://probe_save.json` and `res://probe_settings.json`. Production source and production user saves/settings were not changed. Godot 4.7.2 imported the copy's global classes before both probe scripts ran. Results are `review/reverify_sim_base_results.json` and `review/reverify_sim_extra_results.json`; transcripts are the correspondingly named `.log` files. The engine logged certificate-store access errors; the first import/run also attempted inaccessible engine editor/log files outside the workspace. The tests reached their JSON-output and quit steps. The extra run redirected the engine log into this workspace. These are headless functional tests, not measurements of player usability or renderer performance.

## Additional confirmed defects

| SIM-N01 | P1 | **Restoring a link with Undo while its source is armed permits an accidental replacement whose Undo loses the original.** Normal first-click handling correctly rejects an already-linked source (`main.gd:1274`), so direct ordinary rerouting is not available. The reachable sequence is: select the existing tunnel → Delete (`remove_selected`) → choose DNS → click its now-unlinked source → Ctrl+Z restores the old tunnel, but leaves that source armed → click a different parent → Ctrl+Z. The last step leaves parent=-1/path[] instead of restoring the first tunnel. `reroute_undo_loses_old_tunnel_legal_commands` records pending_after_source=2, restored_parent=1, pending_after_restore=2, new_parent=3 and parent_after_undo=-1. `main.gd:1282`, `1297`, `2311`, `2330`. | Cancel or revalidate armed routing when Undo restores a connection, and recheck the source's one-outgoing-link invariant before committing the second click. If deliberate replacements are introduced, record complete before/after edges. This exact Delete/arm/Undo/target/Undo regression must preserve the original tunnel or reject the replacement without payment. |
| SIM-N02 | P2 | **A same-resource district boundary can bypass its producer-slot limit.** Placement checks the origin region; accounting counts the center region. A 2x2 Phishing footprint with origin region0/center region1 is accepted while region1 has0 slots, then its slots become-1. `main.gd:1182`, `1208`, `1220`. | Choose one owning district for placement and occupancy, or require one-district footprints. Fixture: two adjacent glasshouse regions, destination at cap2, origin in the other region. Placement must reject or debit a consistently defined owner; no district can exceed its declared limit. |
